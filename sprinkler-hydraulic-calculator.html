This page opens every generated report. It sets out the density/area method, the sprinkler discharge equation and the Hazen-Williams friction formula this tool uses.

The remote-area flow is Qremote = density × area. The number of heads operating is N = ceil(area ÷ spacing area per head), and each head is assumed to flow uniformly at Qhead = density × area per head. The system demand at the riser is Qremote + Qhose.

Formulas used

QuantityFormula
Sprinkler dischargeQ = K·√P  ·  P = (Q/K)²
Hazen-Williams friction (US)p = 4.52·Q^1.85 / (C^1.85 · d^4.87) psi/ft  ·  ΔP = p·L
Hazen-Williams friction (SI)p = 6.05·Q^1.85 / (C^1.85 · d^4.87) bar/m  ·  ΔP = p·L
Elevation pressureΔP = 0.433·Δz psi (Δz in ft)  ·  ΔP = 0.0981·Δz bar (Δz in m)
VelocityV = 0.408·Q/d² ft/s  ·  V = 21.22·Q/d² m/s

US units: Q in gpm, P in psi, K in gpm/psi^0.5, d = actual internal pipe diameter in inches, L in ft. SI units: Q in L/min, P in bar, K in L/min/bar^0.5, d in mm, L in m. Minimum operating pressure at any sprinkler: 7 psi (0.5 bar) per NFPA 13.

K-factors (US / metric): 2.8 / 40 (residential ½ in), 5.6 / 80 (standard spray ½ in), 8.0 / 115, 11.2 / 160, 14.0 / 200, 25.2 / 360 (large orifice).

Hazen-Williams C-factors (NFPA 13): unlined cast iron 100, black steel 120, galvanized steel 120, cement-lined ductile iron 140, copper / plastic / CPVC 150. Steel Schedule 40 internal diameters (in): 1 → 1.049, 1¼ → 1.380, 1½ → 1.610, 2 → 2.067, 2½ → 2.469, 3 → 3.068, 4 → 4.026, 6 → 6.065, 8 → 7.981 (ASTM A53 Sch 40; metric = inches × 25.4).

Simplified screening calculation — read before use. This tool models a single remote area with uniform per-head flow and ONE main pipe run from the riser to the remote area carrying the full remote-area flow, plus the head pressure at the most remote sprinkler. It does no branch-line balancing, no node-by-node network solution and no equivalent-length fitting calculation (enter fitting losses as device loss if needed). It is a screening check, not a full hydraulic program. Final design must be by a qualified fire-protection engineer and approved by the AHJ.
