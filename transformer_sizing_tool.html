How to read this: primary-side values (current, cable, conduit, fuse/breaker) are drawn directly from the referenced sizing table — Sizing_2022-05-28.xlsx, tab 2.1, dry-type isolation transformers, primary-side overcurrent protection only, 30°C ambient, with a maximum of three current-carrying conductors per conduit (or equivalent 100% free-air spacing). Secondary-side fuse/breaker maximums are calculated in this tool (not looked up from the table) at ≤125% of rated secondary current per CEC Rule 26-254(2), rounded down to the nearest standard device rating — the table's own primary fuse/breaker columns already show that below 15A a breaker cannot go as low as a fuse can, since 15A is the smallest standard breaker rating. Conduit sizes are shown for both dry (RW90) and wet/underground (RWU90) locations as optional reference — confirm which applies to your installation. Adjusting the impedance field recalculates the secondary short-circuit current only; all other values (conductors, protection, conduit, grounding, panel) are fixed per the source table for the selected rating and do not change with impedance. Typical dry-type transformer %Z from major manufacturers generally falls in the 3.5–6% range for units up to a few hundred kVA (higher for larger/higher-voltage units) — always confirm against the actual nameplate once equipment is selected. This tool is a preliminary sizing aid, not a substitute for a qualified electrical engineer's design or verification against the Canadian Electrical Code and the authority having jurisdiction.
