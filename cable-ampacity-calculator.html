01 · Reference

Cable Ampacity — Lookup & Correction

Look up the base ampacity of a conductor from CSA C22.1 Tables 1–4, then correct it for ambient temperature (Table 5A) and the number of current-carrying conductors (Table 5C).

Base ampacity tables (30 °C ambient, Rules 4-004 / 4-006)

Tables 2 (copper) and 4 (aluminum) give the ampacity of insulated conductors rated 0–2000 V in a raceway or cable, with not more than three current-carrying conductors, at 30 °C ambient. Tables 1 (copper) and 3 (aluminum) give the ampacity of a single conductor in free air.

This tool includes the full Table 2 and Table 4 values (60 / 75 / 90 °C columns) and the Table 1 copper 90 °C column only. The Table 1 60 °C and 75 °C columns and all of Table 3 (aluminum, free air) are shown as “—” (not available) — no verified source was found for them.

Rule 4-006 — termination temperature: the governing column is the lower of the insulation rating and the termination rating. Derating may start from the 90 °C column, but the derated result must not exceed the ampacity of the termination-temperature column.

This tool applies Rule 4-006 by taking the base ampacity and the Table 5A factor from the governing (lower) temperature column, and then capping the corrected result at the termination-column ampacity.

Correction factors

Corrected ampacity = base ampacity × Table 5A factor × Table 5C factor. With parallel sets, the total ampacity is the per-set corrected ampacity × the number of sets.

Rules applied by this tool

RuleWhat it does here
4-004Base ampacity tables are for 30 °C ambient and not more than 3 current-carrying conductors.
4-006Governing column = min(insulation rating, termination rating); corrected ampacity is capped at the termination-column ampacity.
4-028(2)On a 3-phase 4-wire system with non-linear loads, the neutral counts as a current-carrying conductor (triplen harmonics) — the tool adds 1 to the Table 5C conductor count.
8-104Continuous loads are assessed at 125% of the load current: required ampacity = load × 1.25.
