Preliminary sizing aid only. This tool applies the standard NFPA 72 §18.3.7 end-of-line (EOL) voltage drop method with NEC Chapter 9 Table 8 conductor resistance values. It does not include a manufacturer device database — enter each device's worst-case current from the actual UL-listed datasheet. Final voltage drop and NAC loading calculations must be documented and submitted per the AHJ's requirements and reviewed by a qualified fire alarm professional against the specific control panel/power supply manufacturer's own worksheet.
